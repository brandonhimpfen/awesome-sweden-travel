> A curated list of resources for traveling in Sweden, covering official tourism portals, rail networks, regional guides, national parks, and the Right of Public Access.

Sweden is a pristine Nordic destination featuring dense pine forests, thousands of coastal islands, Arctic Lapland wilderness, and the constitutional Right of Public Access. This list is intended for outdoor explorers, wild campers, alpine hikers, and nature conservationists.

## Official & Planning

Essential official government entry portals, tourism boards, Schengen visa rules, and weather tracking.

- [Visit Sweden (Official Tourism Portal)](https://visitsweden.com/) - Official national tourist board guide featuring destination highlights, sustainable travel initiatives, and regional itineraries.
- [Migrationsverket (Swedish Migration Agency)](https://www.migrationsverket.se/English/Startpage.html) - Official government portal for Schengen visa entry requirements, visitor permits, and passport regulations.
- [SMHI Weather (Swedish Meteorological and Hydrological Institute)](https://www.smhi.se/en) - Official national weather service providing forecasts, severe wind warnings, and aurora borealis tracking.

## Transportation & Railways

Navigating national railway networks, intercity high-speed trains, archipelago ferries, and domestic aviation.

- [SJ (Swedish Railways)](https://www.sj.se/en) - Official national railway operator managing high-speed X2000 trains and intercity routes across Sweden.
- [SL Stockholm Public Transport](https://sl.se/en) - Official public transport authority operating Stockholm's metro (T-Bana), commuter rail, trams, and bus networks.
- [Waxholmsbolaget Archipelago Ferries](https://waxholmsbolaget.se/) - Official ferry network operator providing passenger boat services across the Stockholm archipelago.
- [SAS (Scandinavian Airlines)](https://www.flysas.com/) - Major international and domestic carrier connecting Stockholm Arlanda (ARN) with regional airports across the Nordic region.

## Destinations & Regions

### Stockholm & Stockholm Archipelago

- [Visit Stockholm (Official City Portal)](https://www.visitstockholm.com/) - Official municipal visitor guide for exploring the medieval Old Town (Gamla Stan), Vasa Museum, and waterfront districts.
- [Royal Djurgården Administration](https://royaldjurgarden.se/en/) - Official visitor portal for Stockholm's cultural island park, hosting museums, walking trails, and historic gardens.

### Gothenburg & West Coast

- [Gothenburg Tourism Portal (Göteborg.com)](https://www.goteborg.com/en) - Official municipal visitor guide for Sweden's scenic west coast, seafood culinary tours, and archipelago ferry connections.

### Swedish Lapland & Arctic

- [Swedish Lapland Tourism Portal](https://swedishlapland.com/) - Official regional travel hub for exploring Kiruna, Abisko National Park, ice hotels, and northern lights expeditions.

## Allemansrätten (Right of Public Access)

Resources for understanding Sweden's constitutional right to roam, camping guidelines, and outdoor ethics.

- [The Right of Public Access (Naturvårdsverket / Swedish Environmental Protection Agency)](https://www.naturvardsverket.se/en/topics/allemansratten/) - Official government guide outlining public access laws for hiking, foraging, and wild camping in Swedish nature.

## Safety & Emergency Services

Practical advice on emergency contacts, medical assistance, and mountain safety networks.

- [112 Emergency Hotline](https://www.sosalarm.se/) - Integrated national emergency dispatch number for police (Polisen), fire brigade, and medical ambulance services.
- [Mountain Safety Council of Sweden (Fjällsäkerhetsrådet)](https://www.fjallsakerhetsradet.se/) - Official advisory organization providing alpine safety recommendations, weather tracking, and emergency preparation in northern wilderness areas.
